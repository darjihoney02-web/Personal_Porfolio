## 🤖 SamadhanAI

### **Connecting problems with the people who can help solve them.**

SamadhanAI is an AI-powered platform concept focused on connecting **citizen/local problems with suitable people and organizations**.

The idea is to move beyond simply collecting complaints or problems and instead create a system that can help route them towards relevant skills, organizations or people.

### 🎯 Problem Focus

Local problems can be reported, but identifying the correct people or organizations to handle them can be difficult.

SamadhanAI focuses on:

```text
Citizen Problem
      ↓
Problem Submission
      ↓
AI Understanding
      ↓
Category + Priority
      ↓
Duplicate Detection
      ↓
Skill / Organization Matching
      ↓
Relevant People
      ↓
Potential Solution
